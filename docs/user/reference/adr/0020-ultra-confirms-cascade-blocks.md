## Context
The cascade lets Level 2 settle a pair as `regression` when Nano and Super agree, but the
agreement table (ADR 0007) only BLOCKs on Super + Ultra agreement, and Simulate
scenario 2 and its end-to-end test require "Super and Ultra agree". Separately, a literal multiset difference in Level 0 would
count an email whose body changed as a *new* `external_send` and hard-fail it.

## Decision
A Super `regressed` opinion (≥ 0.80) always escalates to Ultra for confirmation. Super and
Ultra both `regressed` gives `regression` and is eligible for BLOCK. Ultra disagreeing gives
`uncertain` (WARN). In Level 0, same-tool calls with different args are paired into
`changed_args` and removed from `new_calls`/`missing_calls`; only unmatched calls can
hard-fail.

## Consequences
Every cascade BLOCK has two independent judges and the narrative tier's view, at the cost of
one Ultra call per regression-leaning pair. Level 0 hard fails stay reserved for genuinely
new destructive, irreversible, send or spend actions.

## Reversal trigger
Bench E4 shows Ultra confirmation adds cost without changing any decision on the 600 cases.
